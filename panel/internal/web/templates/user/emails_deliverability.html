What are SPF, DKIM, and DMARC?

SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
Specifies which mail servers are authorized to send email on behalf of your domain. Prevents spammers from forging the "From" address.
DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
Adds a digital signature to outgoing messages, allowing receiving servers to verify that the email was not tampered with in transit.
DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)
Tells receiving servers how to handle emails that fail SPF or DKIM checks (reject, quarantine, or allow).
