## How to contribute to VietPanel

We welcome and appreciate all contributions - technical or not!

You don’t need to be a developer to make a meaningful impact. Here are some great ways to get involved:

* **[Edit](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/openpanel-translations/issues) or [create new translations](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/openpanel-translations?tab=readme-ov-file#how-can-you-provide-a-translation-for-openpanel)** to help make VietPanel accessible globally
* **Write a blog post** about your experience using VietPanel and link back to the project
* **[Install](https://github.com/trongtri1512/vietpanel) and test** it on your VPS — report any bugs or errors you find
* **[Improve documentation](https://github.com/trongtri1512/vietpanel/docs/panel/intro/#log-in-to-openpanel)** to help others get started: simply click on 'Edit this page' link at the bottom of page
* **Support others** on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/7bNY8fANqF) or in the [Community Forums](https://community.openpanel.org/)
* **[Report UI bugs](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/OpenPanel/issues/new/choose)** directly using the **"Report a Bug"** link at the bottom of the page in VietPanel or VietAdmin UI
* **Test integrations** like [cPanel](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/cPanel-to-OpenPanel) account import, [WHMCS](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/openpanel-whmcs-module), [FOSSBilling](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/FOSSBilling-OpenPanel), [paymenter.org](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/openpanel-paymenter.org) and more

💡 **As a thank you, active contributors can apply for a free lifetime Enterprise license** — our way of saying thank you for supporting VietPanel's growth!

Jump in — every bit helps!
